## Brand & Style

This design system embodies an institutional-grade financial intelligence environment. It blends the ruthless information density and real-time operational posture of tier-one quant and capital-market terminals with a sovereign, security-focused computing architecture. The interface is engineered for institutional analysts, algorithmic treasurers, and privacy-conscious operators managing complex cash flows, automated webhooks, and AI-inferred ledger transformations.

The aesthetic fuses radical brutalist-terminal mechanics with cybernetic dark-mode execution. There are zero rounded corners, zero playful cushions, and zero decorative gradients. Visual hierarchy is established through relentless structural alignment, razor-sharp 1px bounding boxes, dark obsidian container tiers, and intense chromatic telemetry: cold phosphor indigo, radioactive verification emerald, and electric cyber cyan. The user experience conveys sovereign computational autonomy, unyielding precision, and zero-tolerance data clarity.

## Colors

The palette operates on absolute dark luminance containment. Background and container tiers form an obsidian foundation that eliminates visual fatigue while maintaining an impenetrable contrast ratio for dense numeric columns.

- **Primary (`#818CF8` - Cold Indigo Phosphor):** Dictates system commands, core telemetry status, external orchestration ingress (e.g. n8n pipelines), and focused interactive bounds.
- **Secondary (`#00E5FF` - Cyan Phosphor):** Strictly indicates automated synthetic cognition, Gemini LLM vector inferences, conversational assistant dialogues, and query predictions.
- **Tertiary (`#00F59B` - Neon Emerald):** Reserved for deterministic rule verification, cryptographic ledger balance assertions, positive monetary deltas (`+18.4%`), and completed transactions.
- **Neutral Foundation (`#070A0F` to `#1F2C42`):** A strict tiered structure ranging from void black canvas (`#070A0F`) to structural card backgrounds (`#111927`) and elevated modal planes (`#1F2C42`).

Accent colors are deployed as sharp, pinpoint signals against dark surfaces. Large swathes of bright background are strictly forbidden; light values are solely carried through foreground typography, 1px border lines, and low-opacity pill matrices.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy enforces absolute structural discipline. Space Grotesk directs high-level architectural framing, section titles, and uppercase structural headers with aggressive, technical precision. Space Mono governs all ledger transactions, body descriptions, telemetry badges, code payloads, and numerical values.

All monetary figures, balances, and data points must enforce uniform column boundaries. Space Mono inherently preserves columnar alignment across massive transaction sets. Currency ISO tokens (e.g. `USD`, `LKR`, `EUR`) must appear in subdued slate `#8B9BB4`, preceding bright white values (`#E6EDF5`). Percentage deltas must include explicit positive or negative polarity signs (`+4.25%`, `-12.10%`) with dedicated tertiary emerald or error pink colors.

## Layout & Spacing

This layout architecture prioritizes extreme computational density. Wide expanses of decorative margins are eliminated in favor of a 12-column terminal grid governed by a 4px baseline sub-grid. Containers utilize compact 12px to 16px internal padding, allowing operators to monitor multi-panel feeds, transactional tables, and metric arrays within a single screen view.

The layout adapts across form factors:
- **Desktop & High-DPI Terminals (≥ 1180px):** 12-column grid, 12px gutters, 24px outer safe margin. Fixed or collapsible command rail, synchronized multi-column ledger view, and a continuous telemetry stream.
- **Tablet Workstations (896px to 1179px):** 8-column layout, 12px gutters. Secondary data payloads shift to accessible drawers.
- **Mobile Handheld (< 895px):** 4-column layout, 8px gutters, 12px outer safe margin. Tables switch to horizontally pinned monospaced cards with right-aligned numeric anchors.

## Elevation & Depth

This design system explicitly rejects blurred drop shadows, diffuse glows, and pseudo-skeuomorphic layering. Depth is established through hard planar juxtaposition and razor-thin boundary lines.

Visual separation relies on two mechanisms:
1. **Tonal Planar Shift:** Containers rise through slate stepping: `surface-base` (`#0B0F17`) sits flat on `background` (`#070A0F`), while active cards occupy `surface-container` (`#111927`), and focused or interactive drawers occupy `surface-container-high` (`#182335`).
2. **Crisp 1px Hairline Borders:** Every individual widget, input, and module is framed by a 1px border. Inactive cards use `border-subtle` (`#1B2636`). Hovered or focused containers immediately step up to `border-strong` (`#26354A`) or snap to a solid 1px primary accent stroke (`#818CF8`).

Ambient color glows are restricted strictly to micro-status beacons (such as active ingestion signals or conversational AI tool state indicators) and must not exceed a 6px spread.

## Shapes

The shape system is strictly non-rounded. Every component, card, button, tag, drawer, tooltip, and input field is built with absolute 90-degree orthogonal corners (`border-radius: 0px`).

The pure rectangular geometry emphasizes raw digital telemetry, hardware-level certainty, and industrial robustness. Modals, chips, and transaction rows assemble into a cohesive grid of solid digital panels, reinforcing the terminal aesthetic.

## Components

### Buttons & Command Triggers
- **Primary Action Button:** Solid `#818CF8` surface, pure `#070A0F` monospace bold text, 0px corners, 34px height. On hover: rapid background shift to `#9DA7F9` with no transform or blur.
- **Terminal Auxiliary Button:** Background `#111927`, 1px border `#26354A`, text `#E6EDF5`. On hover: border snaps to `#00E5FF`, text changes to `#00E5FF`.
- **Destructive Command Button:** Background `rgba(255, 77, 109, 0.08)`, 1px border `rgba(255, 77, 109, 0.3)`, text `#FF4D6D`. On hover: background shifts to `rgba(255, 77, 109, 0.2)`.

### Metric Containers & KPI Decks
- Framed in 1px `#1B2636` with a `#111927` planar background.
- Header row contains an uppercase `Space Grotesk` title (`10px`, tracked at `0.08em`) in `#8B9BB4`, flanked by a right-aligned delta badge (`+3.4%`).
- Body displays a large `Space Mono` numeric amount in high-contrast `#E6EDF5`.
- Top edge features a 2px horizontal status line: `#00F59B` for verified balance, `#00E5FF` for AI forecasted velocity, and `#818CF8` for automated ledger tasks.

### Transaction Tables & Ledger Rows
- Header height: 32px, background `#0D131D`, border-bottom 1px `#1B2636`. Text rendered in `label-caps` in `#8B9BB4`.
- Row height: 42px. Alternating rows maintain dark slate tone.
- On row hover: background shifts to `#141D2B`, and a 2px vertical neon emerald (`#00F59B`) line activates along the left border.
- Monetary amounts are strictly right-aligned with fixed tabular spacing.

### Status Badges & Classification Chips
- **Deterministic Rule Verified (`RULE_MATCH`):** Background `rgba(0, 245, 155, 0.08)`, border 1px `rgba(0, 245, 155, 0.28)`, text `#00F59B`. Prepended by a solid square telemetry glyph (`■`).
- **AI-Inferred Fallback (`AI_INFERRED`):** Background `rgba(0, 229, 255, 0.08)`, border 1px `rgba(0, 229, 255, 0.28)`, text `#00E5FF`. Prepended by a synthetic diamond glyph (`◆`).
- **Pending Batch Classification:** Background `rgba(251, 191, 36, 0.08)`, border 1px `rgba(251, 191, 36, 0.28)`, text `#FBBF24`.
- **Security Redacted PAN Token (`[REDACTED]`):** Background `rgba(139, 155, 180, 0.12)`, border 1px `rgba(139, 155, 180, 0.30)`, text `#94A3B8`. Used for card numbers and account identifiers.

- Height: 34px. Background `#0B0F17`, 1px border `#26354A`, text `#E6EDF5` in `Space Mono`.
- Focused state: 1px continuous outline in `#818CF8`. Zero ambient halo. A monospaced block cursor (`█`) indicates text entry.
- Quick Query Prompt Chips: 24px height, background `rgba(129, 140, 248, 0.05)`, border 1px `rgba(129, 140, 248, 0.25)`, text `#818CF8`.

### Checkboxes & Selection Inputs
- Square 14px boxes with 0px corners, 1px border `#26354A`, background `#070A0F`.
- Selected state: Interior filled with solid `#818CF8` and a black terminal crossmark (`✕`).
